# EXAMPLES

Here are some example avatars and worlds to get you going.   You can copy and modify if you want to.

## Avatars

#### Egg

The simplest avatar to make is a single 3D object.  Take a look at the egg avatar:

[avatar.properties](https://owingsbj.github.io/WebWorld/view.html?file=avatars/Egg/avatar.properties) -- the properties of the avatar  
[EggAvatar.js](avatars/Egg/EggAvatar.js) -- the JavaScript source for creating the avatar

#### Humanoid

A more complicated avatar has a head, arms and legs, plus is associated with a behavior so it moves with animation.  Take a look at the Humanoid avatar:

[avatar.properties](https://owingsbj.github.io/WebWorld/view.html?file=avatars/Humanoid/avatar.properties) -- the avatar properties for the Humanoid avatar.  
[Humanoid.js](avatars/Humanoid/Humanoid.js) -- the JavaScript source for the Humanoid avatar.  
[metal-skin.png](avatars/Humanoid/metal-skin.png) -- the texture used for the skin of the avatar.  
[eye.png](avatars/Humanoid/eye.png) -- a texture used for the eye of the avatar.

## Worlds

#### BlockWorld

 Take a look at the source for BlockWorld to see how to create the objects of a simple world and handle avatar events to allow building.
 
 [world.properties](https://owingsbj.github.io/WebWorld/view.html?file=worlds/BlockWorld/world.properties) -- the properties of the world.  
 [BlockWorld.js](worlds/BlockWorld/BlockWorld.js) -- the JavaScript source for the world.
